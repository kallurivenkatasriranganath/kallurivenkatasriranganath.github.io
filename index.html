Background

About

Prof. Kalluri V. S. Ranganath is a faculty member in the Department of Chemistry, Institute of Science, Banaras Hindu University, Varanasi. His academic work focuses on designing functional materials and catalytic systems for sustainable chemical transformations.

With a deep passion for science and a commitment to excellence, he strives to bridge fundamental research with real-world impact. As a mentor and teacher, he is dedicated to inspiring curiosity, nurturing young minds, and fostering an environment where innovation and integrity go hand in hand.

Research Themes

Research & Lab Interests

Heterogeneous Catalysis

Development of reusable catalyst systems for efficient organic and biomass-derived transformations.

Functionalized Materials

Surface-modified nanomaterials, polymeric materials, and hybrid catalytic platforms.

Green Chemistry

Sustainable protocols involving biomass conversion, waste-derived materials, and cleaner reaction pathways.
